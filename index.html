The Background

Leadership, resilience, and curiosity in code.

Hi! I'm Shivansh Dhanuka, currently pursuing a BSc in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence at BITS Pilani, while advancing my software engineering craft through Scaler Academy.

Prior to university, I graduated from the American International School of Guangzhou (AISG) (Class of 2025), where I was elected as Secretary of the Student Council, served as Tang Dynasty Captain, played on the Varsity Volleyball team, and served as Model UN Treasurer.

Balancing student leadership alongside rigorous academic commitments and competitive sports taught me the value of time management, perseverance, and clear communication. Those experiences fundamentally shape how I approach software: writing clean, intentional code, building collaborative solutions, and designing web interfaces that truly serve people.

Today, my technical focus spans frontend engineering with HTML5, modern CSS (Flexbox & Grid), TypeScript, JavaScript, algorithmic programming in Python, Java, and C++, and exploring the frontiers of Artificial Intelligence.
